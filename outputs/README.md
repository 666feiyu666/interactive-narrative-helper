# Generated workspace

outputs/ is a disposable workspace for generated indexes, optional local run
traces, workbook previews, figures, tables, and reports. Nothing in this
directory is an authoritative raw source, canonical research dataset, formal
knowledge release, or research version.

Current raw acquisition and deterministic cleaning evidence is retained under
corpus/catalog/ and the ignored corpus/restricted-sources/ tree, not here.

The display MVP may generate:

    outputs/app/educational-design-helper-mvp/
      indexes/
      runs/

Generated content may be removed and rebuilt when its producing method still
exists. Commit only a deliberately selected research output together with its
method, inputs, provenance, and limitations.
