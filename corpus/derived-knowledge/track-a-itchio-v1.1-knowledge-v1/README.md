# Track A itch.io v1.1 knowledge snapshot v1

This frozen snapshot contains 122 model-facing `case_precedent` cards promoted
by the v1.1 scoped substantive-OR policy.

- 2 cards are complete-core Tier B precedents.
- 120 cards are partial-substantive Tier C precedents.
- `knowledge-cards.ndjson` is the approved normalized runtime input.
- `manifest.json` records policy and rule versions, workbook hashes, artifact
  hashes, counts, review method, and limitations.

Partial cards retain `not_stated` or `uncertain` dimensions and turn missing
information into limitations and design questions. Twenty-three cards do not
name a specific mechanic; they retain an explicit interactive narrative form
and leave mechanic selection open.

The cards contain no source descriptions, evidence excerpts, URLs, or local
paths. The snapshot passed a deterministic second-pass audit but has not
undergone independent human double-coding and does not establish learning
effectiveness.

The older strict two-card snapshot remains preserved under
[`../track-a-itchio-v1.0-knowledge-v1/`](../track-a-itchio-v1.0-knowledge-v1/).
