# itch.io Track A codebook v1.0

This codebook defines the normalized labels emitted by
`itchio-track-a-coding-rules/v1.0`. Labels describe creator positioning and are
not outcome measures.

## Educational purpose

- `history_and_cultural_learning`
- `language_and_literacy`
- `health_and_wellbeing`
- `social_emotional_learning`
- `science_and_environment`
- `civic_and_social_issues`
- `professional_skills_and_training`
- `digital_and_media_literacy`
- `mathematics_and_logic`
- `arts_and_creative_practice`
- `religion_and_philosophy`
- `safety_and_risk_awareness`

A purpose label requires an educational action and the labeled topic in the
same local statement. A topic mention elsewhere on the page does not qualify.

## Intended audience

- `children`
- `adolescents`
- `young_adults`
- `school_students`
- `students`
- `educators`
- `parents_and_families`
- `higher_education_students`
- `professionals`
- `adults`
- `age_specified_learners`

Audience coding requires directing language such as “for,” “aimed at,”
“intended for,” “designed for,” “suitable for,” or an equivalent learner-use
statement. Characters and subject populations are not target audiences.

## Application setting

- `classroom_or_school`
- `lesson_or_curriculum`
- `workshop_or_training`
- `museum_or_exhibition`
- `higher_education`
- `home_or_self_directed`
- `community_or_public_program`

The setting must describe intended use. Production contexts, jams, assignments,
and statements about the creator's own course are excluded from this field.

## Interactive narrative form

- `interactive_fiction`
- `visual_novel`
- `choice_based_story`
- `branching_narrative`
- `text_adventure`
- `narrative_simulation`
- `role_playing_narrative`

These labels may use creator-controlled `genres_raw`, `tags_raw`, or explicit
description text.

## Mechanics

- `choices`
- `branching`
- `multiple_endings`
- `dialogue`
- `role_play`
- `simulation`
- `exploration`
- `puzzles`
- `reflection`
- `resource_management`

Mechanics help describe how a precedent works. They do not independently prove
an interaction–education relationship.

## Interaction–education relationship

- `<mechanic>_supports_learning`: the description explicitly connects the
  mechanic to learning, practice, understanding, reflection, awareness, or
  perspective-taking.
- `interaction_and_education_co_described`: mechanics and educational purpose
  are both explicit, but their causal or pedagogical relationship is not.

The latter is a normalized Tier B observation and must be presented with a
transfer limitation.
