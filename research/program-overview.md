# Interactive Storytelling Research Program

This workspace groups research on interactive narrative, AI-mediated
storytelling, and the relationship between generative freedom and authored
rules.

## Current research direction

The program studies whether a generative agent can act as a counterfactual
narrative design partner when authors adapt classic children's stories into
multi-ending interactive narratives. The agent should help an author explore
alternative character actions and project their consequences for plot,
endings, theme, and educational meaning.

## Existing research instrument

The independently versioned `Fox-and-Crow` application is the first playable
testbed. It remains a research instrument and demonstrator rather than the
definition of the whole research program.

## Program boundary

The project distinguishes author-facing design-time assistance from runtime
story narration. General media-generation and interactive-runtime tools remain
outside this repository unless they are brought in through an explicit project
contract.
