# Research figures

This directory contains source-editable figures used in the public research
documentation.

- `full-program-framework.svg` presents the complete Interactive Narrative
  Helper program, including the broader evidence landscape, Track A, the
  human-reviewed design brief, Track B story-level narrative technique
  exploration, author-controlled development, and separate evaluation
  boundaries. Its Track B technique space is conceptual and does not indicate
  implemented modules or progress states.
- `current-study-framework.svg` presents Track A as a method-independent,
  iterative research cycle organized around the research questions, scope, and
  evidence boundaries. It intentionally excludes specific platforms,
  techniques, counts, and progress states.

The SVG files are the canonical figure sources. Keep all public labels in
English, preserve accessible titles and descriptions, and update the figure and
the relevant research text together when scope or status changes.
