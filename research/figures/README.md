# Research figures

This directory contains source-editable figures used in the public research
documentation.

- `full-program-framework.svg` presents the complete Interactive Narrative
  Helper program, including the broader evidence landscape, Track A, the
  human-reviewed design brief, Track B, and separate evaluation boundaries.
- `current-study-framework.svg` presents the narrower operational study based
  on the frozen itch.io candidate inventory and public creator project pages.

The SVG files are the canonical figure sources. Keep all public labels in
English, preserve accessible titles and descriptions, and update the figure and
the relevant research text together when scope or status changes.
