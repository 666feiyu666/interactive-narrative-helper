# Ethics and Rights

## Source stories and corpus material

- Record provenance and rights status for every source.
- Public-domain status applies to an underlying tale, not automatically to a
  modern translation, illustration, adaptation, or game.
- Do not commit copyrighted full texts, game packages, or third-party assets
  without documented permission.
- Keep restricted raw sources outside Git; prefer structural annotations and
  minimal evidence excerpts.
- Do not send protected corpus material to an external model without explicit
  authorization and a documented data-handling decision.

## Human participants

Author studies require consent, privacy, withdrawal, retention, and reporting
plans. Studies involving children require an appropriate institutional ethics
process, guardian consent and child assent where applicable, age-appropriate
procedures, and a clear minimization of collected data.

## Interpretive responsibility

Themes and educational purposes are interpretations. Agent outputs must not be
presented as culturally neutral, universally appropriate, or pedagogically
effective without evidence and qualified human judgment.

## Secrets and operational data

Never commit API keys, `.env` files, private URLs, credentials, participant
data, or identifiable interaction logs.
